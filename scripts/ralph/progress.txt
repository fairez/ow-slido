## 2026-10-16 - US-017
- `lib/note-analysis-ai.ts`: `analyzeNotes` builds the versioned prompt (`notes-v1`), sends approved note text only as `<note id="n1">` data (angle brackets escaped, instruction to ignore instructions inside notes), validates summary (<=150 words), 3-8 categories (fewer only when under 3 notes), up to 50 keywords.
